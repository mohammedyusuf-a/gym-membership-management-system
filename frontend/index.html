Titan Iron Club Code & Safety Rules

01

Strict Iron Etiquette

Always re-rack all Olympic plates, dumbbells, and barbells to their assigned racks immediately after completing your sets. Leaving loaded bars is strictly prohibited.

02

Chalk & Hygiene Policy

Chalk blocks are available in Zone A. Keep chalk inside the chalk bowl. Wipe down benches and machines after your set using disinfectant spray and clean gym towels.

03

Proper Lifting Footwear

Flat-soled shoes, Olympic weightlifting shoes, or barefoot deadlifting on rubber platforms are encouraged. Slippers, crocs, or open sandals are not permitted on the gym floor.

04

Filming & Respect Culture

Tripods are permitted for lifting technique analysis and Instagram creator content, provided they do not block walking pathways or infringe on other members' privacy.

Champion Workout Splits (Beginner to Elite)

Push / Pull / Legs (PPL)

6 Days / Week

The gold standard hypertrophy routine used by competitive bodybuilders and athletes.

  • Mon: Push (Chest / Front Delts / Triceps)
  • Tue: Pull (Upper Lats / Upper Back / Biceps)
  • Wed: Legs (Quads / Hamstrings / Calves)
  • Thu-Sat: Repeat Push / Pull / Legs
  • Sun: Rest & Sauna Recovery

Arnold Antagonist Split

High Volume

Superset pairing opposing muscle groups for blood volume pump and dense growth.

  • Day 1 & 4: Chest & Back Supersets
  • Day 2 & 5: Shoulders, Biceps & Triceps
  • Day 3 & 6: Heavy Squats & Deadlifts
  • Daily: Core & Abs Circuit
  • Rest: Active stretching & mobility

Traditional Tamil Akhada Split

Desi Conditioning

Rotational core torque, shoulder stability, grip endurance, and lung stamina.

  • Day 1 & 3: Teak Karlakattai 64 Swings routine
  • Day 2 & 4: Heavy Brass Gada (Mace) Rotations
  • Day 5: Manila Rope Climbing & Farmer Walks
  • Day 6: Traditional Wrestling Dand & Baithak
  • Sun: Desi herbal oil bath recovery

Club Terms, Safety Rules & Medical Waiver

Required for all active gym members

1. Code of Iron & Facility Etiquette

Members must re-rack all Olympic plates, dumbbells, and barbells to their dedicated racks immediately after completing their sets. Unloaded bars and dropped dumbbells outside designated drop platforms are strictly prohibited.

2. Cleanliness & Footwear Protocol

Clean athletic footwear (flat-soled lifting shoes or Olympic weightlifting shoes) must be worn at all times. Members are required to use personal sweat towels and wipe down benches with antibacterial spray after use.

3. Medical Fitness & Liability Waiver

I confirm that I am medically fit to engage in rigorous resistance training, high-intensity cardio, and sauna facilities. I voluntarily assume full responsibility for my health, personal fitness choices, and safety within the club premises.

4. Membership Terms & Fair Use

Memberships are non-transferable. 30-day medical freeze is permitted on annual memberships with prior written notice. Gym Membership Management System reserves the right to terminate memberships for violations of the iron code.
